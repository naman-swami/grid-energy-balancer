# NERC BAL-001 Real Power Balancing Standard

## Area Control Error (ACE) Equation
Under North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) standards:

$$\text{ACE} = (NI_A - NI_S) - 10 B (F_A - F_S) - I_{ME}$$

Where:
- $NI_A$: Actual net interchange (MW)
- $NI_S$: Scheduled net interchange (MW)
- $B$: Balancing Authority frequency bias (MW / 0.1 Hz)
- $F_A$: Actual system frequency (Hz)
- $F_S$: Scheduled system frequency (60.0 Hz)
